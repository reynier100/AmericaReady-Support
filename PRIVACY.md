# Privacy Policy for America Ready: Life Skills

**Effective date: September 13, 2026**

America Ready: Life Skills (“America Ready,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) is designed to work privately on your iPhone.

## Data We Collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. No account is required.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Learning progress, preferences, language choice, streaks, achievements, and optional reminder settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us.

If you enable a daily learning reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

## Audio

Spoken lessons use audio features provided by Apple on the device. America Ready does not record your voice or collect audio.

## Support Communications

If you voluntarily email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that email so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Support emails are not sold or used for advertising.

## Children and Families

America Ready is an educational app for teens, adults, and families. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not send personal data to third-party services. Apple may process information related to downloading or using apps under Apple’s own privacy policy; that processing is controlled by Apple, not America Ready.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because app progress is stored only on your device, you can remove it using the reset option in Settings or by deleting the app. Support emails may be retained only as reasonably necessary to answer requests and maintain support records.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, email: uscivicspractice@gmail.com

## Independent Educational Product

America Ready is an independent educational app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or any government agency.
